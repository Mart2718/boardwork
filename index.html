A SUPPORTED WAY IN

Help Me Enter

Anyone can say: “I need an entry point.”

  1. Locate. Point to the first word, symbol, or step that needs explaining.
  2. Show. A teammate explains that piece with a small example or sketch.
  3. Try together. Work through one small decision together.
  4. Reconnect. Show where it fits in the original problem.

Can’t locate the confusion? Ask: “Could we explain the question, try a simpler example, or both?”

Still unclear? Ask the instructor or tutor for a starting example. Seated participation, pointing, and sketching are welcome.
